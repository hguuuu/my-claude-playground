# Phase 3 — Marseille + I Ching Casting (Months 7–9) · PLACEHOLDER (re-plan at P2 exit)

## Theme & goal

The contamination-risk phase: add a second, *structurally different* tarot method (Marseille) without corrupting RWS; begin I Ching casting practice; acquire epistemic self-defense before ever reading for others; refine 八字 with the advanced classic.

## Prerequisites

- RWS 78-card fluency substantially achieved (P2 exit)
- Dummett thesis internalized (so Marseille's non-scenic pips are understood historically)

## Reading list

### Yoav Ben-Dov, *Tarot: The Open Reading* + CBD Tarot de Marseille deck — Marseille entry
- **Why chosen:** the accessible English entry to Marseille method; deliberately different from RWS — "no spread positions and no fixed card meanings," meanings emerge from image, gaze, gesture, color, composition, and context. Alternatives: Jodorowsky/Camoin school (French track below), Enrique Enriquez (avant-garde), Paul Marteau (1930s color symbolism — later).
- **Prepare:** purists note Ben-Dov's own pip meanings partly borrow from RWS — use his *method*, watch for that. Trump-order difference: Marseille Justice VIII / Strength XI.
- **Method discipline:** Marseille work lives in its own weekly block, never the same day as RWS spread work.

### Alejandro Jodorowsky, *La Voie du Tarot* (French) — the other Marseille school, in the original

### Hilary Barrett, *I Ching* — practical casting guide
- **Why chosen:** teaches casting (coin + yarrow), changing lines, and carrying a reading into life. Alternative: Karcher *Total I Ching* (mythic register). Wilhelm/Baynes on the shelf as the received-text reference — knowing its Jungian/Neo-Confucian framing.

### Richard J. Smith, *The I Ching: A Biography* — outside view
- The accessible history of the text's evolution from Bronze-Age oracle to world classic; pairs against Barrett exactly per the inside/outside method.

### 《滴天髓》 — 八字 refinement layer
- **Prepare:** terse, aphoristic, "for intermediate-and-above" — you now qualify. Read as a layer that *challenges* 子平真诠, not blends with it (体/用 levels — per your own prior synthesis).

### Ray Hyman cold-reading literature + Forer/Barnum effect — non-magic, **prerequisite for reading others**
- **Takeaway:** recognize when apparent accuracy is subjective validation; identify Barnum statements in your own past readings. Also: Cambridge Element *Divination* for current scholarship.

## Practice

- RWS daily draw continues unchanged; Marseille open-reading practice in its own block
- Weekly I Ching casting, logged per-hexagram
- Historical-event readings with known outcomes (rigorous feedback loop)
- 命例 verification continues with 滴天髓 lens added to the 错题本

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Marseille spread read via open method **without importing RWS keywords** (record and self-audit)
- [ ] Precise articulation of how RWS and Marseille differ as *methods* (teach-back)
- [ ] Cast a hexagram, locate it, identify changing lines, log it
- [ ] Explain Barnum effect + subjective validation and find them in a sample reading
- [ ] 滴天髓 vs 子平真诠: state one point where they disagree and at what level (体/用) it resolves

## Warning signs — **the critical ones**

- **Contamination:** Marseille pips read with RWS scene-narratives (or reverse) → revert to RWS-only 2–4 weeks, re-separate, retry
- I Ching casting mechanics still unclear after a month → more Barrett, less theory
