# Phase 4 — Astrology Start + Lenormand (Months 10–12) · PLACEHOLDER (re-plan at P3 exit)

## Theme & goal

The keystone phase: formal Western astrology begins (unlocking decans → Thoth, Le Tarot Astrologique, deep Golden Dawn correspondences). Lenormand's combinatorial grammar is added now that tarot intuition can be consciously switched off. The East–West comparative project gets its scholarly foundation (Granet).

## Prerequisites

- Tarot mentorship near completion; two tarot methods independently stable
- 八字 intermediate (子平真诠 read; 滴天髓 begun)
- Time budget check: Brennan is 100+ lecture hours — confirm capacity before starting

## Reading / courses

### Chris Brennan, *The Hellenistic Astrology Course* + book *Hellenistic Astrology: The Study of Fate and Fortune*
- **Why chosen over alternatives:** Hellenistic astrology is explicitly a *reconstruction from primary sources* — chart skill **and** historical depth in one curriculum, matching the sourcing values of this whole plan. Alternatives: Steven Forrest (modern/psychological, gentler, less historically rigorous), Kepler College (institutional, 130-week diploma — only if professional), Faculty of Astrological Studies (UK), STA (horary specifically — later if wanted).
- Pair with **Demetra George, *Ancient Astrology in Theory and Practice*** as the practice manual.

### Lenormand trio
- **Rana George, *The Essential Lenormand*** (readable, lineage-rich) + **Andy Boroveshengra, *Lenormand: Thirty-Six Cards*** (rigorous technique: houses, Near-and-Far, Grand Tableau) + **Caitlín Matthews, *Complete Lenormand Oracle Handbook*** (historically grounded, exercises).
- **Why self-study first:** mature, consensus curriculum; compact 36-card system with codified meanings — books suffice to reach competence; formal courses exist if wanted later.
- **Method discipline:** literal keyword *combination* grammar (noun+adjective; no reversals) — the opposite of tarot intuition. Never read Lenormand "intuitively."

### Marcel Granet, *La Pensée chinoise* (French) — correlative cosmology
- The scholarly key to the 八字↔易经 bridge; ancestor of all correlative-cosmology scholarship.

## The parallel-study protocol (astrology ∥ 八字)

Advisable *for you specifically* because the comparative structural research is already your method. Rules to keep them reinforcing, not blurring:
- Maintain the **two-column comparative table**: Western form | Chinese form | **where the analogy breaks**
- Never study the two in the same session with the same vocabulary; comparison is a *third*, separate activity
- Known break-points to record: 五行 phases (生/克) ≠ four elements (hot/cold/wet/dry qualities); modalities ≠ 地支 relations; GD decans ≠ 神煞 derivations; 七政四余 ≠ Hellenistic seven planets

## Warning signs — **the critical ones**

- **Bleed:** astrology/八字 concepts merging (地支 treated as zodiac signs; 神煞 imported into Western charts) → **pause astrology**, re-block until each is independently articulable
- Lenormand drifting into tarot-style intuitive reading → drill Boroveshengra technique
- Brennan course stalling from overload → slow the reading tracks; the course is the phase's anchor
