# Phase 1 — RWS Application + Foundations (Months 1–3) · CURRENT

## Theme & goal

**Adjusted for headstart:** card *recognition* is largely done; this phase builds the *production* half — turning rough card knowledge into applied readings on real questions and real life. Secondary goals: establish the daily practice habit, lay 八字 foundations, and acquire the scholarly vocabulary that frames all five systems.

## Prerequisites

- RWS mentorship underway (met — majors 0–7 done, 8–14 this session)
- A deck, and the journal system set up (Obsidian vault per file 07)

## Reading list — why each book, what to prepare, what to take away

### Rachel Pollack, *Seventy-Eight Degrees of Wisdom* — inside view, **deepening reference**
- **Why chosen:** the standard RWS-interpretation text ("Bible of Tarot"); mythological/psychological/symbolic depth per card, vs. competitors: Robert Place (really a history of the RWS deck — doubles as light history), Katz/Goodwin *Around the Tarot in 78 Days* (workbook — use as drill companion if wanted), Benebell Wen (encyclopedic reference — see below).
- **Headstart usage:** don't read cover-to-cover as primary learning. Read each card's chapter *after* it appears in the mentorship or in a daily draw, to deepen what you've already worked with.
- **Takeaway:** the Fool's Journey as an interconnected arc; per-card depth beyond keywords; feed the per-card notes.

### A.E. Waite, *The Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1911) — primary source
- **Prepare:** Waite is deliberately cryptic (Golden Dawn oaths); minors are thin keyword lists. This documents *what Waite intended* — it does not teach reading.
- **Takeaway:** the designer's own frame for each image; note where his text and the actual Pamela Colman Smith image diverge.

### Benebell Wen, *Holistic Tarot* — reference shelf, not cover-to-cover
- Strong where Pollack is thin (method, court cards). Look things up.

### Wouter Hanegraaff, *Western Esotericism: A Guide for the Perplexed* — outside view
- **Why chosen:** the map of the *academic field* — vocabulary (correspondences, rejected knowledge, Faivre's four characteristics) that locates everything in Phases 2+. Alternative: Goodrick-Clarke's *Western Esoteric Traditions* (more content-survey; optional complement).
- **Prepare:** Hanegraaff is not a practitioner and is skeptical of the "unbroken ancient tradition" narrative — that skepticism is the point.
- **Takeaway:** hold "the Western esoteric tradition" as a scholarly construct; the fact/legend distinction.

### Richard J. Smith, *Fortune-tellers and Philosophers* — outside view (Chinese map)
- **Why chosen:** the best scholarly study of divination as a living social institution in China; frames 八字 and 易经 together.
- **Prepare:** skip-read Ch. 1 (superficial survey); Ch. 2–3 (Qing orthodox cosmology, mantic techniques) are the payload.
- **Takeaway:** correlative cosmology as the connective tissue of all Chinese mantic arts.

### 韦千里《千里命稿》 — 八字 vernacular bridge
- **Why chosen:** Republican-era plain-baihua lectures, systematic on 干支/五行/十神/排盘 with worked 命例 — the recommended on-ramp *before* classical Chinese texts. Alternatives: 袁树珊《命理探原》 (also good, denser).
- **Takeaway:** fluent 排盘 and 十神 identification; a first stock of worked charts.

### Short: Ouspensky tarot essay (taster primary source) · Jung's foreword to Wilhelm's I Ching (first non-magic reading — synchronicity frame)

## Practice (the production engine)

- **Daily 3-card timeline draw** (past/present/future): interpret fully **before any book** → at least one falsifiable statement → verify same evening/next day → log per template (file 07).
- **Weekly:** one fuller spread on a real question; one 排盘 drill by hand from the 万年历.
- **Mentor sessions:** bring attempted readings that felt wrong or ambiguous, cards whose textbook meaning you can't reconcile with the image, and structural questions (e.g., Strength/Justice VIII–XI swap). Not card content — you have that.
- **Knowledge base:** create per-card records for each week's cards; start literature notes; start `STATUS.md`.

## Exit criteria (test now — headstart may already pass some)

- [ ] Blind production: coherent, specific 3-card reading aloud on a real question, no books — twice, two weeks apart
- [ ] Narrate the Fool's Journey and locate any major within it
- [ ] Articulate what Hanegraaff means by "Western esotericism" as a scholarly construct (2-min teach-back)
- [ ] 阴阳五行 生克 relationships and 天干地支 五行 attributes on sight
- [ ] Hand-cast a 八字 chart from the 万年历 (十神 assignment checked by teacher)
- [ ] ≥6 weeks of daily draws logged with predictions + outcomes
- [ ] Mentor confirms readiness for spread-focused work ("am I there yet?" conversation held)

## Warning signs — pause/repeat

- Still reaching for the book on every card after ~8 weeks of daily draws
- Treating meanings as fixed dictionary entries rather than contextual
- Predictions consistently vague/unfalsifiable (Barnum-flavored) — tighten before advancing
