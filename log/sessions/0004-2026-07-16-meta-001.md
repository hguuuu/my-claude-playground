## Why (decisions)

- **Two-layer separation as the core design** — directly supported by two verified precedents (NATO independence doctrine; CERQual grading findings above documents) and consistent with the constitution's never-collapse principle.
- **Observable anchors over judgment words** — the single most documented failure of Admiralty-style scales is that humans cannot apply judgment-worded levels consistently; both graded dimensions here are matters of fact, subjectivity is quarantined into flags + free text.
- **Keep unconfirmed sources** (researcher directive) — register keeps E-grade and 证据不足 material as labeled leads; deletion is information loss, down-labeling is not.
- Tier-2 because this adds a schema layer (provenance schema adjacency).
